AI Fluency Check

Find out how well you work with AI tools, and get a short learning plan for the skill that needs the most practice. Twelve questions across four abilities.

DescriptionExplaining a task clearly to AI
DelegationDeciding what AI should and shouldn’t do
DiscernmentJudging whether AI output is right
DiligenceUsing AI safely and responsibly

Private by design: your answers are scored in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Built by Nuhamin H. Tesfay from the Cybersecurity & AI Capital Model (CCM), research published in the Proceedings of the Southeastern INFORMS Conference 2026. The four abilities follow the AI Fluency framework by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller.

This is a learning tool, not a certified test. Version 1.1, November 2026.
