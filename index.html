Four abilities make someone fluent with AI

DescriptionExplaining a task clearly to AI
DelegationDeciding what AI should and shouldn’t do
DiscernmentJudging whether AI output is right
DiligenceUsing AI safely and responsibly

These four abilities follow the AI Fluency framework by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller, applied in the Cybersecurity & AI Capital Model (CCM).
