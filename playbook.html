AI Readiness Toolkit

Take a small business or nonprofit from “we have AI tools” to “our team uses them safely and well” in four weeks. No AI or IT specialist needed: one facilitator runs it.

The 4-week plan

Week 0
Check and choose. Everyone takes the 15-minute readiness check. Pick 2–3 frequent, low-risk tasks where AI can help most.
Week 1
Diligence
Safe use first. Share the safe-use rules before anyone uses AI for work. Practice sorting requests into “OK for AI” and “not OK.”
Week 2
Description
Clear requests. Task, context, audience, format. Rewrite real tasks as strong requests.
Week 3
Delegation
What to hand off. Break real work into steps and decide what AI drafts, what a person does and what a person checks.
Week 4
Discernment
Catch the mistakes. Find the errors in planted AI answers, then retake the readiness check and compare.
Why safe use comes first: people start using AI on day one, so the rules have to come before the skills.
